Welcome to the NGL

Hello,

Thank you for creating your NGL Club Hub account. You’re one of the first clubs to join us.

We’re a British Gymnastics club too, and the NGL is our way of connecting the things every club juggles: lesson plans, awards, competitions, judges and parents. Here’s what’s ready for you now, and what’s coming next.

Now
What you can do today
 Explore Discover NGL
See every pathway, from Pre-School to Performance League, and how lesson plans, awards, competitions and the parent app link together.
 Reserve competition places
Reserve places for the 2027 NGL General League and Performance League, and keep track of every booking from your NGL Club Hub.
 Plan your classes with the free Rotation Planner
Set up your class rotations so every coach knows where each group should be, with a live screen for the hall.
 Use NGL Club Support
Ideas, partner offers and support to help your club Operate, Coach, Care, Grow and Connect, including parent support with our partner Working With Parents in Sport.
 Grow your judges
Register your judges and young judges on the NGL Judging Pathway, for real competition experience alongside their British Gymnastics qualifications.
January 2027
NGL Recreational launches
 The Recreational Pathway goes live
A term of lesson plans in minutes, built around your apparatus, class lengths and levels, with skill videos and set-up photos. Print it with one click or run it live on a tablet.
 Award books, medals and certificates
Four books, Ruby to Indigo, one level a term, ordered straight from your NGL Club Hub.
 The parent app
Families see what their child is learning each fortnight and how it links to their assessment and competition routine.
 Performance League: coming 2027
For gymnasts training over 10 hours a week. Register interest in your NGL Club Hub and we’ll tell you first.
Through 2027
The rest of the year
 The 2027 NGL General League season
Live-scored competitions with paid judges, for gymnasts training 2 to 10 hours a week.
 NGL Recreational competitions
Ruby to Indigo, built from the same skills as the award books, where every gymnast medals.
 NGL Schools
A licensed schools programme your club can deliver in local primary schools, finishing with a Graduation.
 Coming soon: Pre-School, University League and Masters
The final pathways, so there is an NGL route for every gymnast at every age.
