FROM JANUARY AT SAADI GYMNASTICS

Our Recreational programme is joining NGL

Dear Parents,

From January, our Recreational classes will be rebranded under the National Gymnastics League (NGL) banner as NGL Recreational.

This is part of a wider plan to connect all of our pathways more closely, so that training, testing and competitions link together. For gymnasts who would like to compete, what they practise in class leads straight into their assessments and competition routines.

Our Recreational competitions are all about participation and fun. More structure and targeted coaching will make them even better, and give those who wish to compete the chance to do so for the first time. Gymnasts are, of course, very welcome not to take part.

What isn’t changing

SAADI isn’t changing. It’s the same club, the same coaches and the same classes, and class fees and times are not changing.

We have been talking with British Gymnastics about developing a best-in-class competition network and awards scheme, and this is the first stage.

What this means for your child

✓Even more consistent coaching. Every class already follows the same planned programme. New technology and clearer session plans will make that more consistent still.
✓Sessions built around the group. Over the next few weeks, you may see our coaches using iPads in class. They’re using our new dynamic coaching software to tailor each session to the ability and level of the gymnasts in front of them. Our coaches have always adapted sessions to their group, but the new software makes this much easier.
✓A greater focus on progression towards assessment and competition skills. Gymnasts will know exactly what they’re working towards, and why they’re doing each preparation exercise.
✓See it before they try it. At the start of the lesson, coaches can show the children a video of the skill they’re working towards, so they can see exactly what they’re aiming for. It makes every class more engaging, and helps them understand why each step matters.
✓Better communication with you through the new NGL Families app.

The NGL Families app

Exclusive early access

The app launches in January, but as a SAADI family you can start using it now, two months before anyone else.

The app is connected live to this term’s session plans, so you can:

  • See what your child is learning in class, and when
  • See the skills on their award-book assessment and in their competition routine
  • Record your child’s progress, so you’ll always know their book and level, even if the book goes missing

As far as we know, this is the first app of its kind: connected live to the lesson plans being used in the gym on the day.

Record each level as your child passes it, with the date. Add brothers and sisters too.

Do I need to buy new books?

No. There’s no need to renew your child’s book. When they naturally need their next book, they’ll simply move onto the new NGL Recreational book.
