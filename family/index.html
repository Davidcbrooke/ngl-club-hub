Your NGL Family Hub

Results, league tables and your gymnast's NGL family apps, all in one place. Add your club's family code once and your apps open straight from here.

Register with your club's family code

Your club gives you a family code for each NGL pathway your gymnast is on, for example SAADI-7K4P. For Pre-School, paste the family link your club sent you.

Your codes are saved on this phone or computer only. No account, and we don't store any details about you or your child.

Your family apps

See what your gymnast is learning, their awards and what comes next. Each app opens with your club's code.
